# Design — Housewarming

Concept: **the room and its blueprint.** The real room (Aholo splat) is shown as-is; the simulation
reveals itself as a cobalt blueprint drawn over it — white technical linework, the robot and the Lux3D
furniture as the only solid things. Warm terracotta marks what you can act on (doors, drawers, the robot).
